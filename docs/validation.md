## Payments

Ask Paystack and Peach Payments for written answers covering South African merchant/subaccount eligibility; provider onboarding and KYC; direct provider settlement; split support on stored-card and recurring charges; fee ownership; full/partial refunds and chargebacks; settlement exports and identifiers; webhook authentication and event identity; retry guarantees; test environments; SaaS billing separation; and marketplace attribution. Obtain a payments counsel assessment of the actual contractual and money-flow model. If splits fail, record Option 2 and the limits on marketplace checkout/commission. Do not assert a registration exemption from technical routing alone.

## Video and cross-border processing

Ask the candidate video vendor for storage, processing, backup, CDN and licence-service locations; applicable subprocessors and DPA; analytics controls and mandatory IP/device processing; deletion and retention; signed URL expiry during active playback; seamless refresh on supported browsers/devices; DRM licence lifetime and renewal; audio-only restrictions; resumable upload behaviour; and pricing. Test renewal, revoke-while-playing and slow/offline recovery before claiming the ten-minute bound. Have privacy counsel assess the whole pipeline, including CDN, auth, email and payments. Record first enterprise customer's residency requirements.
