Please provide your current DPA/sub-processor register and written answers on video storage/encoding/backups and region choice; viewer IP/device/licence processing locations; optional versus mandatory Mux Data analytics and controls to minimise/disable them; content/viewer-data retention and deletion; signed URL expiry during active playback; refreshing playback and DRM tokens without interruption; DRM licence duration; audio-only DRM limitations; and the CDN/delivery path. Assess identifiable people in media, viewer IP/device information, analytics, CDN and DRM processors, contractual protection and required notices/permissions. Confirm responsible-party/operator roles and whether the operator agreement needs additional transfer safeguards. Include the first enterprise buyer's residency constraints.
