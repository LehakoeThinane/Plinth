# Hub purpose and consent schedule — draft

| Purpose | Hub scope | Intended control | Open decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Required account/access and purchased delivery | Current hub plus necessary global identity | Explain required service processing; do not present mandatory contract processing as optional marketing consent | Confirm lawful basis and controller/operator roles |
| Provider marketing | Single hub | Optional, separate unchecked opt-in; revocation as easy as grant | Copy, channels, retention and evidence |
| Company-assigned progress reporting | Assigned employer seats/content only | First-sign-in disclosure; employer's processing basis recorded | Company agreement and notice |
| Public community contributions | Current hub/space | Explain audience before posting and effects of deletion | Notice and moderation retention |
| Optional playback analytics | Approved purpose only | Default minimisation; optional tracking decision assessed separately | Vendor data and lawful basis |
| Marketplace/cross-hub discovery | Future Model C | New purpose and explicit sharing decision; no implicit consent transfer | Deferred |

Record user, hub, purpose version, notice version, granted/revoked times and acquisition channel. A deletion or revocation must update affected downstream processing; retaining consent evidence requires a defined purpose and period. Counsel must approve the final bases and notices.
