# Operator agreement — draft for counsel review

Status: unsigned working draft. Provider and platform legal identities, contact details and governing terms remain to be filled in. This draft does not constitute approval to process production data.

## Scope and roles

The provider instructs the platform to operate its hub, deliver purchased content, manage membership, communicate service events and administer company seats. Responsible-party/operator roles must be confirmed separately for provider member data, employer-directed training data and the platform's own accounts/billing/security. The platform does not assume every activity is processing solely on the provider's behalf.

## Processing schedule

Categories: account identifiers, membership and consent records, employer seat assignments, learning progress, community/messages and attachments, order/invoice evidence, device/session security logs and identifiable people in uploaded media. Purposes, permitted recipients, retention and regions are listed in the processing schedule and sub-processor register. Special personal information is excluded by default until a documented lawful basis and safeguards are established.

## Instructions and confidentiality

Process only for documented purposes and approved instructions. Staff access is limited to role and time-limited support access with reason and audit. Sub-processors require review, contractual safeguards and a maintained register. No reuse of learner data for cross-hub marketing or training models is implied by hub membership.

## Safeguards and assistance

Apply tenant and company isolation, encryption, controlled secrets, version-checked entitlements, incident monitoring, backups and tested restoration. Assist with access/correction/export/deletion requests, legal holds and regulator enquiries. Notify the provider immediately on reasonable grounds of an unauthorised access/acquisition event; preserve evidence and cooperate with responsible-party notification obligations. Define contacts, escalation and operational response targets in the incident schedule.

## Transfers and end of service

No cross-border path is accepted without its assessment and contractual basis. At termination or valid deletion, return/export data and apply record-specific disposal, including copies, caches, search and processor requests. Required retention is purpose-limited, restricted and auditable; backups expire on schedule and restored data has deletion tombstones reapplied. Downloaded learner copies cannot be recalled.

## Review checklist

Counsel must settle roles, legal bases, transfer mechanism, liability, incident duties, support access, sub-processor change notice, retention, dispute/termination terms and signatures. Fill in identities and contacts before use.

Source: [Information Regulator security safeguards guidance](https://inforegulator.org.za/knowledge-base/category/popia/chapter-3-conditions-for-lawful-processing/part-a-processing-of-personal-information-in-general/condition-7-security-safeguards/).
